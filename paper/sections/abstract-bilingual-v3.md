## 中文摘要

开放式遥感变化描述与变化问答旨在用自然语言解释双时相影像中的地表变化，其可靠评价是比较和改进生成模型的基础。现有 BLEU、CIDEr、METEOR 和 ROUGE-L 等参考文本指标主要衡量候选输出与有限参考表达的相似程度，因而难以区分事实保持改写与实体、时相方向或空间位置错误。我们的核心思路是把评价目标从“文本是否像参考答案”转化为“语言中的原子变化声明能否在双时相图像中找到一致证据”。实现这一思路需要同时处理双时相关系验证、语义标签类别有限以及开放词汇视觉定位噪声，并在证据缺失时区分事实反驳与不可验证。为此，本文提出 Mask-Guided Alignment（MGA），通过领域 Claim 解析、MGA-GT/MGA-OV/MGA-Hybrid 逐实体证据路由以及 Add、Remove、Modify 和 source-to-target 关系构造，分别输出 Spatial、Temporal、Coverage 和 Verifiability 诊断。我们在 LEVIR-MCI 和 SECOND-CC 上开展三评审人工先导实验、200 场景 600 条事实图受控评测、覆盖全部 64 个类别子集的标注可用性曲线，以及小批量变化问答和事实保持风格改写实验。结果表明，随着可用语义类别从 0% 增加到 100%，MGA-Hybrid 的 Neutral AUC 从 0.522 提升到 0.944、Balanced Accuracy 从 0.553 提升到 0.935，同时可评分覆盖率从 0.915 提升到 1.000 且 Unverifiable Rate 从 0.085 降至 0，说明 Hybrid 能在可靠标注与开放视觉证据之间形成可解释且可扩展的覆盖—准确性折中。

## English Abstract

Open-ended remote-sensing change captioning and change question answering seek to explain surface changes in bi-temporal imagery with natural language, making reliable evaluation essential for comparing and improving generative models. Existing reference-based metrics such as BLEU, CIDEr, METEOR, and ROUGE-L primarily measure similarity to a limited set of reference expressions, and therefore struggle to distinguish claim-preserving rewrites from errors in entities, temporal directions, or spatial locations. Our central insight is to replace the question of whether an output resembles a reference with whether its atomic change claims can be supported by consistent evidence in the image pair. Realizing this idea requires bi-temporal relation verification, support for entities outside a limited semantic label space, robustness to noisy open-vocabulary localization, and an explicit distinction between contradiction and insufficient evidence. We introduce Mask-Guided Alignment (MGA), which combines domain claim parsing, entity-wise MGA-GT/MGA-OV/MGA-Hybrid evidence routing, and Add, Remove, Modify, and source-to-target relation construction to report separate Spatial, Temporal, Coverage, and Verifiability diagnostics. We evaluate MGA on LEVIR-MCI and SECOND-CC through a three-rater human pilot, a fact-graph-controlled benchmark with 200 scenes and 600 language samples, an exhaustive label-availability curve over all 64 semantic-class subsets, and small-scale change-QA and claim-preserving style-rewrite studies. As semantic-label availability increases from 0% to 100%, MGA-Hybrid improves Neutral AUC from 0.522 to 0.944 and Balanced Accuracy from 0.553 to 0.935 while increasing evaluation coverage from 0.915 to 1.000 and reducing the Unverifiable Rate from 0.085 to zero, demonstrating an interpretable and extensible coverage–accuracy trade-off between reliable annotations and open-vocabulary visual evidence.
